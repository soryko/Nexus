# Correlation probe findings — Antigravity App 2.35.0, 2026-11-24

Run by Gemini inside the owner's Antigravity App at the owner's request, with the probe plugin
installed temporarily and uninstalled afterwards. The raw log was left on the owner's machine
(`~/.gemini/antigravity/plugin_data/nexus-correlation-probe/correlation-probe.jsonl`); it is not
committed. Conversation ids below are shortened to their first 8 characters.

## What was run

Five `probe_echo` calls: `a1`, `a2`, `a3` in one conversation, `b1` in a second conversation, and
`s1` from a subagent of the first. `analyze.py` verdict: `conversation_key_found`.

## Observed

| Call | Hook `conversationId` | `_meta` `conversation_id` | `_meta` `root_conversation_id` | `_meta` `parent_conversation_id` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a1, a2, a3 | `fb21cc62` | `fb21cc62` | `fb21cc62` | absent |
| b1 (second conversation) | `c6a550c5` | `c6a550c5` | `c6a550c5` | absent |
| s1 (subagent of `fb21cc62`) | `9df31c5b` | `9df31c5b` | `fb21cc62` | `fb21cc62` |

- `_meta` keys on every `tools/call`: `antigravity.google/artifacts_dir`,
  `antigravity.google/conversation_id`, `antigravity.google/root_conversation_id`, `progressToken`,
  plus `antigravity.google/parent_conversation_id` for the subagent.
- **`conversation_id` equals the hook's `conversationId` in all five calls**, subagent included.
  `root_conversation_id` differs inside a subagent.
- No per-call identifier reaches the request: the hook's `toolCall.id` (`call_<digits>`) and
  `stepIdx` are not in `_meta`. (The analyzer's `stepIdx == rpc_id` hit is one coincidental match
  out of five and is excluded from its verdict by design.)
- `initialize` advertised no `roots` capability. Capabilities: `extensions`
  (`com.google.antigravity/ui` entrypoints `aux_pane`, `global`, `file`;
  `io.modelcontextprotocol/ui`) and `elicitation`. `clientInfo`:
  `antigravity-client v1.0.0`; protocol version `2026-07-28`.
- One MCP server process (the hub language server's child) served both conversations and the
  subagent. Two server starts were logged because MCP servers were refreshed twice during setup.
- Pre-hook payload keys: `artifactDirectoryPath`, `conversationId`, `cwd`, `hookEventName`,
  `lastUserInput`, `modelName`, `stepIdx`, `toolCall` (`id`, `name`, `args`), `transcriptPath`,
  `workspacePaths`.

## Consequence for the gateway

Leases are bound to `_meta["antigravity.google/conversation_id"]`, falling back to
`root_conversation_id` only when the former is absent. A request consumes only a lease written by
a hook of the same conversation. Binding is per conversation, not per call: the host sends no
per-call key.

## Limits

- One App session on one machine. The CLI and IDE were not observed here (the CLI documents
  `root_conversation_id` from v1.4.4). A full App restart was not part of the run.
- Side effect seen: after uninstalling the probe, the still-open conversation kept the probe's hook
  registered and every tool call in it failed until a new conversation was started.
